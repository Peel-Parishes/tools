Before you post a photograph Written consent first, every time, for everyone recognisable. For anyone under 18 that means a parent or guardian has signed the form, and it must be on file before the photo is taken — not chased afterwards. Never put a child's name in the same post as their face. If you can't remember whether someone has consented, don't post it.
Words to go with it Write like you'd say it to someone at the door. No exclamation marks stacked up, no "Don't miss out!!". Put the important thing in the first line — the rest gets hidden behind "more".
